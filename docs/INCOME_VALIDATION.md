# INCOME VALIDATION — the measurable gates
*(2026-08-23. Correctness is proven by the test rigs; whether people
PAY can only be proven by people paying. This plan turns "income
worthy" into four gates with numbers, so the answer is measured,
not felt. Track every entry in `pilot_ledger.csv` beside this file.)*

## The one honest premise

No more code can validate income. The product is technically
validated (21/21 independent checks at 1M rows, zero fabricated
numbers across every audit). What's unvalidated is: will a stranger
with a messy file pay money for this output. Each gate below puts
the product in front of people with less patience and more money
than the last, and each has a pass number decided IN ADVANCE.

## Gate 1 — the testers (running now, free)

Your QA/test guys' feedback forms (TESTER_GUIDE.md question 5:
"Would you pay for this report? What's missing before you would?").
- PASS: 3+ of the testers say they'd pay $25 or more for the report
  on THEIR OWN file; no unfixed wrong-number reports.
- FAIL tells you: what's missing is named in their answers — fix
  the top named gap and re-ask. Do not proceed to strangers with a
  known "I wouldn't pay because X" unaddressed.

## Gate 2 — five real pilots (this week, $0–25 each)

Five real businesses with real files — your network, the testers'
contacts, one local shop. Cheap or free ON PURPOSE; the price of
admission is their honest answer to two questions after delivery:
"was anything wrong or confusing?" and "what would you pay for this
monthly?"
- PASS: 3+ of 5 accept the report without a confused follow-up
  call; 2+ name a monthly price of $40 or more; zero numbers
  challenged that turn out wrong (challenged-and-right is GOOD —
  the receipts settle it and that story sells).
- Log per pilot: minutes of YOUR time (intake + owner check +
  delivery — the wall-clock lines print the machine's share),
  what they asked that the report didn't answer.

## Gate 3 — ten paid orders from strangers ($15–95)

Channels per the market research already in GIG_COPY.md: direct +
Zelle first (instant), r/slavelabour + Craigslist + Discord
freelance (no account aging), Fiverr/Upwork posted now as pipeline
(their clearance pays weeks 2–4).
- PASS: 10 paid orders inside 30 days of posting; refund rate 0
  under the guarantee (any unreceipted number = free revision/
  refund); 3+ repeat-or-referral; your time under 30 min/order.
- The unit economics line that decides everything:
  (dollars collected) ÷ (your hours spent) ≥ $25/hr by order ten —
  below that, the price or the time cost must move before scaling.

## Gate 4 — the recurring question (month 2)

One pilot converted to a monthly arrangement (the "department"
offer: their files in, reports back, standing). One recurring
client at $99+/mo who renews once = the product is income-worthy
by the only definition that matters. Then the decision is scale
(more clients, same shape) vs deepen (the engine research).

## Standing rules that protect the income

- Owner reads every report before it ships (OWNER_CHECK.md is the
  5-minute routine). Disclosure line always. Client data local,
  deleted on request, never in any learning system.
- Wrong numbers are never argued — rerun, show the receipt, and if
  the receipt is wrong, refund and file the bug. One honored
  guarantee is worth more marketing than ten testimonials.
- Track wall-clock minutes on everything (owner rule 2026-08-22) —
  income per hour is the real metric, and it needs the hours.
